Systems

What to Add to the Game

Core systems that keep players hooked while staying true to a chill fishing experience.

01

Fish Rarity & Encyclopedia

  • Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic
  • Each fish has size range, preferred bait, time of day, and location
  • Collection book with progress % and set completion rewards
02

Rods, Reels & Baits

  • Rods with stats: Power, Control, Luck, Durability
  • Baits that attract specific types or rarities
  • Shop + crafting progression so upgrades feel meaningful
03

Multiple Islands

  • Starter Lagoon — easy fish, tutorial waters
  • Mangrove Creek — night fish, eels, mystery
  • Coral Reef — colorful rares
  • Storm Isle / Deep Ocean — legendaries, harder conditions
04

Time, Weather & Events

  • Day / Night cycle changes available fish
  • Rain, fog, storms affect bite rate and rare spawns
  • Weekly Golden Hour or limited-time fish events
05

Social & Light Progression

  • Biggest catch of the day leaderboard
  • Simple trading or gifting for duplicate rares
  • Daily / weekly quests (“Catch 5 rare fish at night”)
06

Boats & Exploration

  • Unlock better boats to reach farther islands
  • Hidden fishing spots only reachable by boat
  • Optional light exploration — chests, NPC quests
Core Loop

Recommended Fishing Minigame

A skill-based system that feels rewarding without frustration — works great on mobile and PC.

“Tension & Timing” System

Five clean steps that create a satisfying loop.

1

Cast

Hold to charge power, release to throw. Distance and accuracy matter.

2

Wait

Bobber floats. Bite time depends on bait and location. Visual + sound cue.

3

Hook

Quick tap when the bobber dips. Miss it and the fish escapes. Tighter window for rares.

4

Fight

Keep the marker in the green zone by holding/releasing the reel. Too much tension snaps the line.

5

Land

Size, rarity, and XP shown. Big fish break personal records and give better rewards.

TENSION BAR PREVIEW

Keep the white marker inside the green zone while the fish fights

Why this works on Roblox

  • Simple hold / release controls — perfect for mobile and PC
  • Skill ceiling: better players land bigger and rarer fish more often
  • Easy to tune difficulty per fish rarity
  • Feels active and satisfying instead of pure RNG
Plan

Suggested Content Roadmap

Ship the core loop first, then expand outward.

Phase 1

Core Loop

1–2 islands, basic rods, 15–20 fish, solid minigame, simple shop.

Phase 2

Progression

More islands, better gear, encyclopedia, daily quests, weather.

Phase 3

Social & Events

Leaderboards, limited-time fish, trading, boats, QoL.

Phase 4

Endgame

Legendary hunts, cosmetics, seasonal events, deeper bait & rod systems.

Advice

Quick Design Tips

Keep sessions short-friendly

Players should feel real progress after 10–15 minutes of fishing.

Make rarity feel special

Rare fish need unique animations, sounds, and particle effects.

Balance RNG + Skill

Luck helps, but good players should still catch more and bigger fish.

Mobile first

Most Roblox players are on mobile — keep the minigame simple and readable.
